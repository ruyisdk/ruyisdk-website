# RuyiSDK Team Showcases Posters at RISC-V Summit Europe 2026

RISC-V Summit Europe 2026 was held in Bologna, Italy, from June 8 to 12. The RuyiSDK testing team presented four posters and exchanged ideas with developers worldwide on package management, course development, chip verification, and a browser-native RISC-V toolchain.
